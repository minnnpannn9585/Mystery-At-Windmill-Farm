三 关于此游戏 / About This Game
==================================================

以下为 Steam 格式标记文本。支持格式标记的编辑方式可直接粘贴；若可视化编辑器把 [h2] 等显示成正文，请通过编辑器应用标题、粗体和列表样式，再移除标记。保存后预览中英文页面。

【简体中文】

[h2]一颗蛋不见了，整个农场都有话要说。[/h2]
淑芬那颗画着爱心的宝贝蛋失踪了。

小鸡侦探团似乎已经有了结论，看门狗的记忆却有点靠不住，池塘边的青蛙更是把每句话都说成了谜语。它们究竟看见了什么？又有多少，是自己脑补出来的？

现在，轮到你这只鸭子来理清头绪了。

《风车农场悬案》是一款融合探索、对话与轻推理的单人 3D 冒险。化身绿头鸭侦探芦笛，在一座色彩明快的微缩农场里寻找物证、追问证词，把一场越传越离谱的失踪案，一点点拼回原来的样子。

[h2]听懂动物们的「跨频道聊天」[/h2]
这里的每只动物都有自己的脾气，也都有自己的说法。认真听，接着问，再带着新发现回来：一句信誓旦旦的证词，可能会被一根不起眼的羽毛推翻。冷幽默、误会和煞有介事的推理，藏在一来一回的对话里。

[h2]迈开鸭掌，去现场看看[/h2]
沿着池塘走走，绕到谷仓背后，再跳上高处换个角度。留意羽毛、脚印与那些不太对劲的小细节，找出能让调查继续推进的线索。农场不大，值得你多看一眼的地方却不少。

[h2]让手帐记住你的每一次发现[/h2]
找到的线索、听到的证词和逐渐成形的推测，会随着调查自动记入侦探笔记本。新证据出现，旧结论被划掉；翻回前页，看看自己是从哪一步开始想岔的。

[h2]奶酪到位，情报好说[/h2]
藏在墙缝里的老鼠兄弟有自己的消息渠道，也有自己的价目表。探索途中捡起散落的奶酪碎，换取情报和八卦。至于哪些话值得当真，还得靠侦探自己判断。

[h2]把一个小小的谜团，认真查到底[/h2]
明亮的低多边形农场、个性鲜明的动物邻居，以及一段由荒诞误会串起的故事，等你慢慢翻开。你追查的是一颗蛋，也是在听懂这群动物眼中的世界。

【English】

[h2]One missing egg. Everyone has a story.[/h2]
Mabel's precious egg—the one with a heart drawn on it—has vanished.

The Chick Detectives seem to have solved the case already. The guard dog's memory is a little hazy. And the frog by the pond makes every answer sound like a riddle. What did they actually see, and what did they just imagine?

Time for a duck to sort things out.

Mystery at Windmill Farm is a single-player 3D adventure built around exploration, conversation, and light deduction. Play as Reed, a mallard detective, and investigate a colorful miniature farm. Gather evidence, question the locals, and untangle a missing-egg case that's getting more outlandish with every telling.

[h2]Get to the bottom of some very odd conversations[/h2]
Every animal has a personality, an opinion, and a version of events. Listen closely, ask another question, then return with what you've found. One overlooked feather might unravel a very confident statement. Expect deadpan humor, crossed wires, and some impressively questionable detective work.

[h2]Put your webbed feet to work[/h2]
Wander along the pond, poke around the barn, and hop up high for a different view. Look for feathers, footprints, and little details that don't quite fit. This farm may be small, but there's plenty to make a curious duck stop and look.

[h2]Keep the case in your notebook[/h2]
Your detective notebook automatically records clues, testimony, and emerging theories as the investigation unfolds. Watch old conclusions get crossed out when new evidence arrives, then flip back through the pages to see where your thinking took a wrong turn.

[h2]Good gossip costs cheese[/h2]
The Mouse Brothers have their sources—and their prices. Pick up scattered cheese crumbs as you explore and trade them for information and gossip. Deciding what to believe is still your job.

[h2]Give a little mystery your full attention[/h2]
Explore a bright, low-poly farm and get to know its eccentric residents through a story full of absurd misunderstandings. You're looking for an egg, but you're also learning how each animal sees the world.

[/list]

If you enjoy eccentric animals, deadpan humor, and piecing together a mystery, we'd love to have you along. Add the game to your wishlist, and we'll see you at the farm!
